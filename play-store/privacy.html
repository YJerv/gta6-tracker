Leonida Map & Guide

Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026. This policy applies to the Leonida Map & Guide website and to the paid Google Play edition (package uk.co.yjerv.leonidaguide).

Who we are

Leonida Map & Guide is an unofficial fan companion published by YJerv. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

What we collect

We collect no personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no third-party trackers. We do not operate a backend for this app, and we do not receive a copy of your progress.

What stays on your device

Progress ticks, the spoiler setting, and similar preferences are stored only in your browser (or the Play app’s embedded browser) using localStorage on this device. That data never leaves the device unless you export it yourself from Settings. Clearing the site’s data, uninstalling the app, or using a different device starts you from empty progress.

Google Play

If you buy the Google Play edition, Google processes the purchase and payment under Google’s own policies. This app has no in-app purchases and no ads. We do not receive your payment card details.

Children

The app is a game companion that includes illustrated firearm imagery and fictional crime-themed jobs from the game. It is not directed at children. We still collect no data from anyone who uses it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this page with a new effective date.

Contact
