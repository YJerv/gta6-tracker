# Privacy policy — Leonida Map & Guide

Effective 26 November 2026.

This is the Markdown source of the public policy. The hosted page is `privacy.html` at the site root, published at [https://yjerv.github.io/gta6-tracker/privacy.html](https://yjerv.github.io/gta6-tracker/privacy.html) once GitHub Pages is enabled on this repository.

## Who we are

Leonida Map & Guide is an unofficial fan companion published by YJerv. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## What we collect

**We collect no personal data.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and no third-party trackers. There is no backend for this app, and the publisher does not receive a copy of a player’s progress.

## What stays on the device

Progress ticks, the spoiler setting, and similar preferences are stored only in the browser (or the Play app’s embedded browser) using `localStorage` on that device. That data never leaves the device unless the player exports it from Settings. Clearing the site’s data, uninstalling the app, or switching device starts from empty progress.

## Google Play

If the player buys the Google Play edition (package `uk.co.yjerv.leonidaguide`, £2.99), Google processes the purchase and payment under Google’s own policies. The app has no in-app purchases and no ads. The publisher does not receive payment card details.

## Children

The app is a game companion that includes illustrated firearm imagery and fictional crime-themed jobs from the game. It is not directed at children. No data is collected from anyone who uses it.
